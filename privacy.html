Privacy Policy

TempMail Shelf processes pasted emails, domains, URLs, custom allowlists, and custom blocklists locally in your browser. The static page does not need an account, database, or upload endpoint.

Hosting providers may process ordinary request metadata such as IP address, browser, device, page URL, and timestamp. Do not paste sensitive production data unless you trust the page copy and hosting environment you are using.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure page visits and loading performance. Our analytics integration does not submit your pasted entries, allowlists, or blocklists.
